Biography

I am Daodu Opeyemi Isaac, a graduate of Mechanical Engineering who is building a second professional skill in software development. I joined the School of Engineering at AltSchool Africa to learn software engineering in a structured way, from the foundations through frontend, backend and full-stack development, with a focus on how AI tools are used to build modern products.

Why I Joined the school of engineering program

  1. To strengthen my existing software-development knowledge. Rather than learning randomly from different resources, I wanted a structured curriculum that takes me from software-engineering foundations through frontend, backend and ultimately full-stack development.
  2. To learn how AI can be incorporated into software development. The AI-powered aspect was important because I do not just want to know how to write code. I want to understand how modern developers use AI coding tools and LLM APIs to build products more efficiently. ALT SCHOOL specifically includes tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot and LLM APIs.
  3. To improve my employability and open opportunities beyond Mechanical Engineering. My goal is not necessarily to abandon Mechanical Engineering. Rather, I am developing software as an additional professional skill that can create opportunities in software engineering, freelance work, remote jobs and technology-related roles.
